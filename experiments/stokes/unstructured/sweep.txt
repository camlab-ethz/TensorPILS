# Identical to experiments/stokes/stokes_paper/stokes_benchmark except for the domain, the mesh
# and the preconditioner's velocity half: K=10, 1024/128/256, 500 epochs, batch 32, Adam.
# Settings are STAGE 1's picks on this mesh, not the structured study's, and the difference
# matters: the structured pick omega=256 fails here (test velocity 99.9 / 10.3 / 15.6 % across
# seeds, kept under output/stokes/unstructured/superseded/), while omega=16 gives 1.69 %.
# Stage 1, seed 42, lr 1e-3:
#     omega     0.5      16      64     256
#     velocity  2.58%   1.69%   4.25%  22.94%
#     pressure  1.25%   0.79%   1.62%   3.93%
# omega=16 is also the value CLAUDE.md records as the CONDITIONING optimum for this
# preconditioner used as a norm weight. On the uniform grid the empirical optimum sat far from
# it; here the two coincide.
# data likewise takes lr 1e-3 (2.41 % combined) over the structured pick 3e-4 (2.58 %).
#
# The two baselines of the structured table are absent by construction, not by choice: PINO
# takes finite differences on an image and PI-DeepONet's branch is a fixed sensor grid. That
# absence is the result, and the structured comparison is what makes it one -- keep both tables.
#
